About us

What is Clean-Matrix?

Clean-Matrix is an environmental organisation based at Meru University, Kenya. We work with schools, communities and individuals to reduce waste, restore green spaces and promote sustainable living through practical action.

Where do you operate?

We're based in Meru, but our events and projects reach communities across Kenya, from Nairobi to the coast. Check our events page to find something near you.

How is Clean-Matrix funded?

Our paid services, such as eco-camps, rainwater harvesting installations, online courses and compost sales, help fund our free community clean-ups and awareness events.

Volunteering & events

How do I become a volunteer?

Fill in the form on our volunteer page. We'll reach out with upcoming opportunities that match your interests and availability.

Do I need any experience to volunteer?

Not at all. Everyone is welcome. We provide guidance and any equipment needed at each event, so all you need to bring is enthusiasm.

Are your events free to attend?

Yes. Community events like tree planting, clean-ups and climate talks are free and open to everyone.

What should I bring to an event?

Comfortable clothes and closed shoes, a reusable water bottle, a hat and sunscreen. We'll let you know ahead of time if anything extra is needed.

Services

What services do you offer?

We offer environmental training and workshops, holiday eco-camps, rainwater harvesting installation, an online eco-learning hub, and organic fertilizer and compost. See the full list on our services page.

How do I book a service?

Use the registration form on the services page. Pick a service, leave your details and we'll get back to you with pricing and dates.

Can schools book workshops or eco-camps?

Absolutely. We regularly run workshops and holiday camps for schools and can tailor sessions to different age groups.

How can I buy compost or fertilizer?

Register for the Organic Fertilizer & Compost service or contact us directly to place an order.
